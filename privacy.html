Privacy & Terms

Draft for the pilot at the University of Chittagong. It will be reviewed and updated before wider launch.

What we collect

  • Your name and email, from your Google account when you log in.
  • For verification: your student ID, department and, optionally, your university email.

Who can see it

  • Your student ID, department and university email are visible only to you and the site administrator.
  • We use them only to check that you are a student. We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time. To ask for your data to be removed, contact the site administrator.

Using HATBODOL

  • Give true information. Do not post illegal, harmful or misleading items or services.
  • HATBODOL connects students. Buying, selling, renting and payment happen directly between users, at their own risk. We do not handle payments.
  • Meet in safe public places on campus and check items before paying.
  • The administrator may remove listings or accounts that break these rules.
